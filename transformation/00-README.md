## Realistic expectations (read this honestly)

- **92 → 85 kg in 49 days = 1.0 kg/week.** That is the *aggressive ceiling* of safe fat loss (0.5–1 % of body weight/week per consensus guidance, e.g. ACSM / ISSN position stands). Expect a fast first week (−1.5–2 kg, mostly water/glycogen), then ~0.7–0.9 kg/week. **Likely landing zone: 85–86.5 kg by Sept 4** — and you will *look* leaner than the number suggests because you're keeping muscle.
- **Weight fluctuates ±1–1.5 kg daily** from sodium, carbs, and hydration. Judge only the **7-day rolling average**.
- **Skin:** retinoids need 8–12 weeks for visible texture change. In 49 days expect: better hydration, fewer breakouts, brighter tone, reduced puffiness. Full glow-up continues past Sept 4.
- **Muscle:** in a deficit as a non-beginner you mostly *preserve* muscle; as a relative beginner you can gain some ("recomposition"). Strength on big lifts should hold or climb slightly.
- **What compounds hardest in 49 days:** posture, grooming consistency, haircut/beard lines, clothes fitting a leaner frame, confidence from kept promises. These change how you look *more* than 3 extra kg would.

## Abu Dhabi summer reality check

July–August in Abu Dhabi: 40–48 °C days, humid nights, air quality dips. This plan is built around it:

- **All cardio/steps indoors or pre-sunrise / post-21:00.** Malls (Yas, Marina, Galleria), treadmill, Corniche only after dark.
- **Hydration target is higher than generic advice:** 3.5–4 L/day + electrolytes on training days.
- **Vitamin D is still likely low** — paradoxically common in the Gulf because everyone avoids the sun. Test or supplement (see supplements file).
- **Gym recommendation:** GymNation (~99–169 AED/month, branches across Abu Dhabi) is the best value; alternatives: Active Fitness, Vogue Fitness (pricier). Any gym with a barbell, dumbbells to 40 kg, cables, and treadmills covers this program.
