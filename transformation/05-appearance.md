# PART 10 — APPEARANCE MAXXING (evidence-based only)
# PART 11 — PRODUCT RECOMMENDATIONS (Abu Dhabi prices)

**Honest framing:** the biggest appearance changes in 49 days come from — in order — fat loss (face and jawline included), posture, grooming consistency, fitted clothes, skin hydration + SPF, and sleep. Everything else is a few percent each. This plan does all of it, but know where the leverage is.

---

## 10.1 Skincare

### Morning (5 min, ★★★★★)
1. **Gentle cleanser** (or just water if skin is dry) — CeraVe Foaming or Cetaphil Gentle
2. **Moisturizer** — CeraVe/Cetaphil lotion
3. **Sunscreen SPF 50** — the single most evidence-backed anti-aging product that exists (UV drives most visible facial aging — photoaging literature is unambiguous). Reapply if outdoors midday. **Yes, even though you avoid the AD sun — car windows and 10-minute exposures count.**

### Night (7 min, ★★★★★)
1. Cleanse (double-cleanse on sunscreen-heavy or gym days: micellar water → cleanser)
2. **Retinol** Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun (The Ordinary Retinal/Retinol or adapalene 0.1 % — Differin, available in UAE pharmacies, is the strongest OTC-class option and gold-standard for acne + texture). Start 2×/wk, build up. Pea-sized amount, dry skin, moisturizer after. **Expect mild flaking weeks 1–3 — normal.**
3. Other nights: **niacinamide 10 %** (The Ordinary) — oil control, tone, barrier
4. Moisturize. Under-eyes included.

### Weekly (Sunday, ★★★☆☆)
- **BHA 2 % (salicylic acid)** 1–2×/wk (Paula's Choice or The Ordinary) — pores, gym-sweat congestion. Not on retinol nights.
- Optional clay mask if oily.
- **Never:** physical scrubs on face, DIY lemon/toothpaste hacks, > 2 actives per night.

### Rules that matter more than products
Clean pillowcase 2×/wk · never touch face at gym, wipe equipment, shower within 30 min of training (gym acne prevention) · hands off pimples · introduce ONE new product per week so you can spot reactions.

## 10.2 Body, lips, hands, feet
- **Body:** shower lukewarm (hot water strips barrier — relevant with 2 showers/day in AD summer); moisturize post-shower (Nivea/Vaseline lotion); **bacne:** salicylic-acid body wash on back 3×/wk, shower immediately after gym.
- **Lips:** Vaseline or any SPF lip balm daily; no licking.
- **Hands:** hand cream at night (Nivea/Neutrogena); nails: **cut + file weekly** (Sunday grooming hour), push cuticles after shower, never bite. Clean short nails are a top-3 noticed detail.
- **Feet:** pumice weekly, moisturize; rotate shoes daily (see shoe care).

## 10.3 Hair & scalp
- Shampoo 3–4×/wk (daily sweat rinse with water is fine), conditioner every wash — mid/lengths.
- **Anti-dandruff if any flaking:** ketoconazole 2 % (Nizoral, UAE pharmacies) 2×/wk — strong evidence.
- Cold-ish final rinse; **towel pat, don't rub**; heat styling on medium with distance.
- **Haircut every 3 weeks** (~40–80 AED at a good local barber; book Sep 2 cut for finale). Ask the barber: "what suits my face shape?" — a great cut is the highest-ROI appearance purchase that exists.
- Scalp: don't scratch; sunscreen/cap for exposed partline outdoors.

## 10.4 Teeth, tongue, breath (★★★★★ — smiles are rated in every attractiveness study)
- Brush 2 min, 2×/day, **fluoride**, electric brush (Oral-B) with light pressure; **wait 30 min after acidic food**.
- Floss or interdental brushes nightly — this is what actually fixes breath, plus:
- **Tongue scraping** every morning (metal scraper, ~15 AED) — the tongue is the main odor source.
- **Whitening (safe route):** whitening toothpaste daily + **Crest/Oral-B whitening strips** (available UAE pharmacies/Amazon.ae, ~100–150 AED) — one 14-day course starting Aug 20 finishes right at Sep 4. Avoid charcoal powders (abrasive) and salon "laser" deals of unknown chemistry.
- Coffee through-the-day staining: rinse water after coffee; consider straw for iced.
- Dental cleaning appointment if > 1 year since last (~150–300 AED at UAE clinics) — instant visible upgrade.

## 10.5 Brows, facial hair
- Brows: **tweeze obvious strays only** (between brows, far outliers), weekly. Do NOT reshape/thin — overdone brows read worse than untouched.
- Beard: pick ONE neckline (two fingers above Adam's apple), keep it crisp; trim to even length 2×/wk (Philips trimmer). Sharp lines > length.
- Shave (if clean-shaven): with the grain, after shower, Gillette + gel, moisturize after — eliminates most razor burn.

## 10.6 Posture, neck, forearms, grip (the "carriage" upgrades)
- **Posture (★★★★★):** biggest instant visual change available. Protocol: face pulls + rows already in program · **chin tucks ×10, 3×/day** (set phone reminders) · wall slides ×10 daily · monitor to eye level, elbows 90° · hourly stand-up during work blocks · "string from crown of head" cue when walking.
- **Neck:** farmer's carries + deadlifts + shrug-throughs in program build the traps/neck base safely. Direct neck curls optional (★★☆☆☆): lying neck curls/extensions 2 × 15 light, 2×/wk if desired — go slow, zero ego.
- **Forearms & grip:** wrist curls (Fri) + farmer's carries (Sat) + no straps on rows/deadlifts until grip fails first. Visible in rolled sleeves; grip strength correlates with basically every health outcome.

## 10.7 Face: puffiness, jawline, massage — what's real
- **Real levers for facial leanness:** overall fat loss (this plan) · sodium spikes and alcohol cause morning puffiness — big late salty meals show on your face at 7 am · full sleep · hydration.
- **De-puff protocol (morning, 2 min, ★★☆☆☆ but pleasant):** cold water splash 30 s or chilled spoon under eyes; brief downward face/neck massage with light pressure ("lymphatic drainage" — evidence is thin, temporary effect is real, cost is zero).
- **Jawline:** it is revealed by fat loss + posture (forward head hides jawlines), not created by gadgets. **Chewing:** normal food chewing + occasional sugar-free gum is fine; **hard-chew devices and hours of mastic gum are NOT recommended** — masseter hypertrophy is modest, and TMJ/jaw-joint problems are a real risk. This is the main looksmaxxing myth to skip. Mewing: no credible evidence in adults; skip.
- **Eyes/dark circles:** mostly genetics + thin skin + sleep + allergies. What helps: 7.5 h sleep on schedule, treat allergic rhinitis if present (AC dust — antihistamine), caffeine eye cream (small temporary effect, ★★☆☆☆), retinol reaching under-eye area gently over months, SPF always. What doesn't: expensive "miracle" creams.
- **Eye care generally:** 20-20-20 rule during screen work (every 20 min, 20 s, look 6 m away); artificial tears if AC dries eyes.

## 10.8 Fragrance, clothing, shoes, accessories
- **Fragrance (★★★☆☆):** 2–3 sprays (chest/neck), not more. AD heat amplifies projection — go lighter than you think. Affordable excellent picks in UAE: **Lattafa Asad or Khamrah (~80–130 AED, made in UAE, genuinely well-rated)**, Armaf Club de Nuit Intense (~110 AED). Buy at Lulu/Carrefour perfume aisles or brand stores — avoid street "testers."
- **Clothing maintenance:** Sunday 15′ — steam/iron the week's shirts; lint-roll; check for pilling/stains. **Fit rule: as you lose 6 kg, get 2–3 core shirts tailored (~15–25 AED/shirt at any local tailor) — fitted cheap clothes beat loose expensive ones, always.** Neutral palette (white/black/navy/grey/olive) makes everything match.
- **Shoes:** rotate 2 pairs (dry-out day prevents odor); clean white sneakers weekly (magic eraser/wipes); one leather pair polished monthly. Scuffed shoes are the most-noticed grooming failure in studies of first impressions — allegedly and anecdotally, but cheap to fix regardless.
- **Accessories:** one decent watch, one belt matching shoes, sunglasses that fit your face (try in-store). Less is more.

## 10.9 Confidence habits (the multiplier)
- Confidence in 49 days comes from **kept promises to yourself** — the consistency score in your tracker *is* the confidence program.
- Daily: walk with the posture cue, deliberate slower speech, hold eye contact in conversations one beat longer than comfortable.
- Weekly: one small social rep outside comfort zone (talk to a stranger at the gym, post the video even if imperfect).
- Photos every Sunday — watching your own visible progress is the most honest confidence fuel there is.
