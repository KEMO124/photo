## 3.1 The calculations — shown in full

**Inputs:** Male (assumed), 178 cm, 92 kg, age assumed 27 (recalculate below if different — each ±5 years shifts BMR by only ~±25 kcal, so the plan barely changes).

### Step 1 — BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor — the most validated equation in overweight adults)

```
BMR = 10(92) + 6.25(178) − 5(27) + 5
    = 920 + 1112.5 − 135 + 5
    = ~1,900 kcal/day
```

### Step 2 — TDEE (maintenance)

Activity: desk-based work **but** 5 lifting sessions + cardio + 8–10k steps → activity factor 1.5 (deliberately conservative; people overestimate this more than any other number).

```
TDEE = 1,900 × 1.5 ≈ 2,850 kcal/day  (working estimate: 2,800–2,900)
```

### Step 3 — Deficit

- 7 kg of fat ≈ 7 × 7,700 = 53,900 kcal → over 49 days = **1,100 kcal/day deficit** required for pure fat. That's ~39 % below maintenance — too aggressive to hold muscle and gym performance for 7 weeks.
- **Chosen deficit: ~900 kcal/day (≈ 30 %)** = ~0.8 kg fat/week + first-week water drop of 1–2 kg → lands at **85–86.5 kg**, preserving muscle. Evidence: deficits > ~35 % increase lean-mass loss and adherence failure; ~0.7 % BW/week loss preserves lean mass in resistance-trained dieters.

### Step 4 — Daily targets (★★★★★)

| Target | Amount | Why this number |
|---|---|---|
| **Calories** | **1,950 kcal** (range 1,900–2,000) | 2,850 − 900. Floor at 1,900 — never go below; you're training hard in extreme heat |
| **Protein** | **175 g** (700 kcal) | 2.0 g/kg of *goal* weight (85 kg × ~2.05). ISSN & meta-analyses: 1.6–2.4 g/kg in a deficit preserves muscle & maximizes satiety — the single most protective macro choice |
| **Fat** | **60 g** (540 kcal) | ~0.65 g/kg — enough for hormones (testosterone dips when fat < ~0.5 g/kg) and skin, low enough to leave room for carbs |
| **Carbs** | **~178 g** (710 kcal) | The remainder. Fuels lifting; timed pre/post workout on training days |
| **Fiber** | **30–38 g** | 14 g per 1,000 kcal (Institute of Medicine). Satiety + gut + cholesterol |
| **Sodium** | **2,300–3,000 mg** | You sweat heavily in AD summer + training. Do **not** slash sodium; low sodium + heavy sweating = cramps, dizziness, misery. Avoid only ultra-processed excess |
| **Water** | **3.5–4 L** (+750 ml around workouts) | Baseline ~35 ml/kg + Gulf-summer sweat losses. Urine should be pale straw |

**Adjustment rule:** if 7-day average loss < 0.5 kg for two consecutive weeks → drop 100 kcal (from carbs) or add 2,000 steps. If loss > 1.2 kg/week after week 2, add 100 kcal. Never adjust off a single day's weight.
