## 5.1 Program logic (why it's built this way)

- **5 lifting days (U/L/P/P/L hybrid):** in a deficit, *heavy resistance training + high protein* is what tells the body to keep muscle (★★★★★ — consistent across the diet literature). Each muscle is hit ~2×/week, the frequency sweet spot in volume meta-analyses.
- **Compound-first:** squats, hinges, presses, rows recruit the most muscle per minute and burn the most energy. Isolation work is dessert, not dinner.
- **RPE-based loads:** RPE 7–8 = 2–3 reps left in the tank. In a deficit, chronic RPE 9–10 outpaces recovery.
- **Cardio after lifting, never before:** lifting quality drives muscle retention; cardio is the calorie tool.

**Warm-up (every session, 8–10 min):** 3–5 min incline walk → arm circles, band pull-aparts ×15, cat-cow ×8, bodyweight squats ×10, then **2–3 ramp-up sets** of the first lift (bar → 50 % → 75 %).
**Cooldown:** heart rate down with 2–3 min slow walk → 10-min stretch (section 7.1).

## 5.3 Progressive overload plan

1. **Double progression:** each exercise has a rep range. Hit the top of the range on *all* sets at the target RPE → add weight next week (+2.5 kg upper / +5 kg lower, smallest jump available on machines).
2. **Log every set** (tracker in `07-trackers.md`). No log = no progress signal.
3. **Weekly wave:** Weeks 1–2 base → Weeks 3–4 add 1 set to the first two exercises of each day (peak volume) → **Week 5 deload (60 % sets, RPE 6)** → Weeks 6–7 full volume, aim to beat Week 4 numbers.
4. **In a deficit, "adding a rep" is progress.** Maintaining top-set weight across 7 weeks while dropping 6 kg = you got relatively much stronger.

## 6 — CARDIO (the fat-loss accelerator, not the foundation)

**Priority order: steps > post-lift LISS > one interval session. Diet creates the deficit; cardio widens it.**

| Mode | Prescription | Heart-rate target | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Daily steps** | 8,000–10,000/day. Abu Dhabi summer: mall loops (Yas Mall ~1 km/lap), treadmill, corridor walks, post-sunset Corniche | n/a | ★★★★★ |
| **Incline treadmill LISS** | 3×/wk × 20′ post-lift (Mon/Thu) + Wed 45′ | Zone 2 = 60–70 % HRmax ≈ **115–135 bpm** (HRmax ≈ 220 − age ≈ 193) — you can talk in sentences | ★★★★★ |
| **Cycling intervals** | 1×/wk (Sat): 10 × 1′ hard / 1′ easy | Hard = Z4 ≈ **155–170 bpm**; recovery Z2 | ★★★☆☆ — time-efficient, but > 1×/wk in a big deficit eats recovery |
| **Swimming** | Optional swap for Wed walk — outdoor pools are bath-warm in Aug; use indoor/shaded | Z2 effort | ★★★☆☆ — zero-impact, great if you enjoy it; not required |
| **More HIIT** | Not prescribed | — | ★★☆☆☆ — meta-analyses show HIIT ≈ LISS for fat loss at matched calories; extra HIIT in a 30 % deficit mostly steals lifting performance |

**Weekly cardio schedule:** Mon 20′ LISS · Tue 0 (steps) · Wed 45′ walk/swim · Thu 20′ LISS · Fri 0 (steps) · Sat intervals 20′ · Sun 45–60′ long walk. ≈ 150–170 min/week + steps — matches WHO guidance for health with margin.

## 7 — RECOVERY (where the results are actually built)

### 7.1 Daily stretching (10′ post-gym + 5′ pre-bed)

**Post-gym (30–45 s/side each):** couch stretch (hip flexors — desk-worker priority #1) · hamstring stretch · chest doorway stretch · lat hang on bar · figure-4 glute stretch.
**Pre-bed 5′:** neck side bends + chin tucks ×10 · child's pose 1′ · lying spinal twist/side · deep slow nasal breathing 10 breaths (parasympathetic switch).

### 7.2 Foam rolling (★★★☆☆ — modest evidence for soreness/ROM; use targeted)
Wed & Sun, 8–10′: quads, glutes, upper back (thoracic extension over the roller — feels incredible after desk days), lats, calves. 30–45 s per area, slow passes. Skip lower back (roll glutes/hips instead).

### 7.3 Mobility (Wed & Sun, 15′)
World's greatest stretch ×5/side · 90/90 hip switches ×10 · thoracic wall rotations ×8/side · ankle dorsiflexion rocks ×10 · deep squat hold 3 × 30 s (goblet counterweight if needed) · wall slides ×10 (posture).

### 7.4 Sleep optimization (★★★★★ — sleep restriction cuts the *fat* proportion of weight lost dramatically; see Nedeltcheva et al., Ann Intern Med 2010)

| Lever | Protocol |
|---|---|
| Consistency | 23:00–06:30 daily, weekend drift ≤ 1 h |
| Light | Bright light within 30′ of waking; screens off / dim + warm after 22:15 |
| Temperature | AC at 21–23 °C (yes, even at electricity cost — it's a performance expense) |
| Caffeine | Last coffee 14:00 (~9 h before bed) |
| Phone | Charges **outside** the bedroom. Alarm = cheap clock (~20 AED) |
| Late eating | Dinner done by 20:15; huge late meals fragment sleep |

### 7.5 Stress reduction
- 10 slow nasal breaths (4 s in / 6 s out) before deep work and before bed — the fastest reliable parasympathetic lever.
- The 45′ walk *without* podcast 2×/week — boredom is where your best video ideas will appear.
- One fully-off block per week (Sun 16:30–19:30): no tracking, no content, no optimizing.

### 7.6 Deload rules
- **Scheduled:** Week 5 (Aug 15–21) — 60 % of sets, RPE ≤ 6, no intervals.
- **Emergency deload triggers** (any 2 for 3+ days): resting HR up > 7 bpm, sleep quality tanks, all top sets feel 2 RPE harder, motivation gone, joints ache. Response: 3 light days + calories to ~2,200 for 2 days. This is course-correction, not failure.
